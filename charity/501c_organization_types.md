# 501(c) Organization Types: Descriptions and Deductibility

| Type       | Short Description | Purpose                                                                 | Key Restrictions                                                                 | Donations Deductible |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| 501(c)(1)  | Federal Entities  | Corporations organized under an Act of Congress (e.g., Federal Credit Unions, Federal Reserve Banks). Serve public purposes like banking or housing. | No Form 1024, 990 exempt. Contributions deductible only for public purposes. No Grift. | Yes (public purposes) |
| 501(c)(2)  | Property Holding  | Title-holding corporations for exempt organizations. Hold property and turn over income (less expenses) to a tax-exempt parent organization. | Income must be paid to exempt parent. Limited to property management. No Grift. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(3)  | Charities         | Charitable, religious, educational, scientific, literary organizations, or those testing for public safety, fostering amateur sports, or preventing cruelty to children/animals. Includes public charities (e.g., CHAI) and private foundations. | No Grift. No substantial lobbying (501(h) election allows limited lobbying). No political campaign activity. File Form 1023 once, 990 annually. | Yes                  |
| 501(c)(4)  | Civic Welfare     | Civic leagues or social welfare organizations promoting community welfare (e.g., homeowners associations, volunteer fire companies). | No Grift. Unlimited lobbying if related to purpose. Contributions not tax-deductible unless for specific purposes (e.g., fire departments). File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No (except specific cases) |
| 501(c)(5)  | Labor/Agriculture | Labor, agricultural, or horticultural organizations (e.g., unions, farm bureaus). Promote worker rights or agricultural interests. | No Grift. Lobbying allowed if related to purpose. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(6)  | Business Leagues  | Business leagues, chambers of commerce, or professional associations (e.g., American Solar Grazing Association). Improve business conditions. | No Grift. Unlimited lobbying for business interests. Contributions deductible as business expenses. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No (business expense) |
| 501(c)(7)  | Social Clubs      | Social or recreational clubs (e.g., country clubs, fraternities). Provide leisure activities for members. | Non-discriminatory policy (except religion). Minimal public use (<$2,500/year or <5% receipts). Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(8)  | Fraternal Benefits| Fraternal beneficiary societies operating under a lodge system, providing life, sickness, or other benefits (e.g., Elks). | Must offer insurance/benefits. Non-discriminatory except for religion. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(9)  | Employee Benefits | Voluntary employees’ beneficiary associations providing life, sickness, or accident benefits to members (e.g., labor union benefits). | Membership voluntary, tied to employer/union. No discrimination in benefits. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(10) | Fraternal Charity | Domestic fraternal societies without insurance, devoting earnings to charitable, religious, or educational purposes (e.g., Masons). | No insurance benefits. Funds for exempt purposes. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(11) | Teacher Pensions  | Teachers’ retirement fund associations. Pay retirement benefits to local teachers/school employees. | Funded by taxes, donations, investments. Local scope. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(12) | Mutual Utilities  | Local benevolent life insurance associations, mutual irrigation, or telephone companies. Serve members’ needs. | 85%+ income from members for losses/expenses. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(13) | Cemeteries        | Cemetery companies operated for members or charitable purposes. | No private profit. Funds for maintenance. Contributions deductible if charitable. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | Yes (if charitable)  |
| 501(c)(14) | Credit Unions     | State-chartered credit unions or mutual reserve funds. Provide financial services to members. | State-regulated. No Grift. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(15) | Small Insurance   | Mutual insurance companies or associations (small insurers). Provide insurance to members. | Gross receipts <$500,000. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(16) | Crop Finance      | Cooperative organizations financing crop operations. Support agricultural activities. | Funds for crop financing. No private profit. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(17) | Unemployment Funds| Supplemental unemployment benefit trusts. Pay benefits to employees during layoffs. | Benefits only for involuntary layoffs. No Grift. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(18) | Old Pensions      | Employee-funded pension trusts (pre-1959). Provide pension benefits. | Employee-funded only. No diversion of funds. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(19) | Veterans Groups   | Veterans’ organizations providing benefits or social activities for members. | 75%+ members are veterans. Contributions deductible if 90%+ for exempt purposes. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | Yes (90%+ exempt)    |
| 501(c)(20) | Legal Services    | Group legal services organizations (discontinued after 1992). Provided legal benefits. | Obsolete. No new exemptions. | No                   |
| 501(c)(21) | Black Lung Trusts | Black lung benefit trusts. Fund benefits for coal miners. | Specific to black lung claims. Contributions deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | Yes                  |
| 501(c)(22) | Pension Liability | Withdrawal liability payment funds. Manage employer pension liabilities. | Specific to pension plans. No Grift. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(23) | Old Veterans      | Veterans’ organizations (pre-1880, benefits-focused). Similar to 501(c)(19) but older. | Rare. Contributions deductible if for exempt purposes. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | Yes (exempt purposes) |
| 501(c)(24) | ERISA Trusts      | Trusts for ERISA Section 4049 plans (post-1980 groundwork for organizations with significant grants or foreign expenses). | Specific to ERISA plans. No Grift. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(25) | Multi-Parent Hold | Title-holding corporations with multiple parents. Hold property for exempt orgs. | Up to 35 parents. Income to exempt orgs. No Grift. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(26) | High-Risk Health  | State-sponsored high-risk health insurance organizations. Provide coverage to high-risk individuals. | State-regulated. No private profit. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(27) | Workers Comp      | State-sponsored workers’ compensation reinsurance organizations. Manage insurance risks. | State-regulated. No Grift. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(28) | Railroad Pensions | National Railroad Retirement Investment Trust. Manage railroad retirement funds. | Specific to railroad pensions. No Grift. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 501(c)(29) | CO-OP Health      | CO-OP health insurance issuers under ACA. Provide consumer-oriented health plans. | ACA-specific. No private profit. Contributions not deductible. File Form 1024 once, 990 annually. | No                   |
| 4947(a)(1) | Charitable Trusts | Nonexempt charitable trusts treated as private foundations for charitable purposes. | No Grift. Subject to private foundation rules (e.g., excise taxes, distributions). File Form 1023 once, 990 annually. | Yes                  |

Notes:
- **501(c)(3)** and **4947(a)(1)** are most relevant for charities (e.g., CHAI), with strict rules against private benefit and political activity. Donations are generally tax-deductible.
- **501(c)(4)** and **501(c)(6)** are common for advocacy or business NGOs, with fewer lobbying restrictions but typically non-deductible donations.
- **Donations Deductible** reflects IRS rules: "Yes" for charitable or specific exempt purposes; "No" otherwise, with qualifiers for conditional cases.
- Sourced from IRS Publication 557 and web references for accuracy in nonprofit transparency analysis.
